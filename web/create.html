Unmuddle Use a ready-made lesson

Write your own questions

Any subject works. Ask open questions that learners answer in a sentence or two, and add what a correct answer should say.

Tips for questions that reveal misconceptions

  • Ask learners to explain why or how, not just to name a fact.
  • Pick something beginners often get wrong in a predictable way.
  • Keep the reference answer short: the key idea a correct answer must contain.
