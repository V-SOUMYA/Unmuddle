How it works

Grading tells you who got it wrong. Unmuddle tells you why.

  1. Your group answers in their own words.
  2. AI checks each answer against the lesson's reference answer.
  3. Sentence embeddings group wrong answers that share an idea.
  4. An LLM names each misconception and writes a quick fix for you.
  5. Each learner gets feedback and a check question. Get it right, and their dot turns plum on your screen.
