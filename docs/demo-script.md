# Demo video script (about 3 minutes)

Record the screen at 1280x800 or larger. Have a phone (or a narrow browser window) ready
for the learner side. Use the live site so the QR code works.

## 0:00–0:20 The problem

Show the landing page.

> "When I TA a class of 50, grading tells me who got it wrong, not why. Student leaders
> teaching AI to beginners have the same problem, and beginners pick up AI myths fast:
> 'ChatGPT searches the internet', 'computers can't be biased'. Unmuddle finds those myths
> for you."

## 0:20–0:45 Start a session

Click **Lesson 8: Large language models**. Show the dashboard with the code and QR code.

> "A leader picks a lesson from a 12-lesson beginner AI curriculum. Learners scan the QR
> code. No accounts, no app."

## 0:45–1:15 A learner answers

On the phone, scan the QR code, type a name and answer: "It searches Google and
summarizes the results." Show the dashboard counter going up.

## 1:15–2:00 Find misconceptions

To show a full class, open a second tab and click **Try a demo class** on the landing
page, then **Find misconceptions**.

> "Gemini checks each answer against the reference. Sentence embeddings group wrong
> answers by meaning, so 'browses the web' and 'searches Google' land together even
> though they share no words. Then the LLM names each misconception and writes a
> 2-minute fix."

Scroll through one question. Point at the dots, the title, the 2-minute fix and the
example answers.

## 2:00–2:35 Close the loop

Back on the phone, show the feedback page: the kind note, the idea behind the mix-up,
and the check question. Answer it correctly. Switch to the dashboard and watch one dot
turn indigo.

> "Every learner gets personal feedback and a new question. When they get it right, their
> dot turns indigo, so the leader can see the fix working."

## 2:35–3:00 How we know it works

Show the README results table.

> "We measured it: grouping is scored against hand labels, and we evaluated on Eedi, a
> dataset where experts labeled thousands of real student mistakes. It's tested, it's
> open source, and it's live. Unmuddle: see what your class got wrong, and why."
